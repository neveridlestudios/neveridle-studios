# NeverIdle Studios — Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026 NeverIdle Studios is an independent content creation and publishing project that produces original AI-assisted entertainment videos. ## Information We Process When a TikTok account is connected through TikTok's authorization process, NeverIdle Studios may process the account identifiers, authorization tokens, and publishing permissions necessary to upload videos to that account. We do not request TikTok account passwords. ## How Information Is Used Account information is used to authenticate authorized publishing requests, upload videos, check publishing status, and maintain records that prevent duplicate uploads. ## Storage and Security Authorization credentials are stored in a restricted environment and are not intentionally made publicly accessible. Access is limited to the publishing application and its operator. ## Third-Party Services TikTok account authorization and video publishing are provided through TikTok's APIs and are subject to TikTok's own policies. ## Data Retention and Deletion Publishing records and authorization credentials are retained only as needed to operate the integration. Users may request disconnection and deletion of stored account information. ## Contact For privacy inquiries, contact: NeverIdleStudios@gmail.com ## Updates This policy may be updated as the application and its capabilities evolve.
